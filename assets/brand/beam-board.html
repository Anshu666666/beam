What we heard

The brief

Beam is a high-throughput parallel downloader written in Go with zero dependencies. Its frontend follows a distinct neo-brutalist / retro-editorial chassis: solid color palettes (zero gradients), tactile rivets, 2px solid dark ink borders, hard shadow offsets, and a Windows XP file-transfer metaphor.

tactileconcurrentretro-editorialmechanicalfast

Success: immediate recognition at 16 px favicon, matches the frontend chassis aesthetic, works across dark and light cockpit themes, zero gradients.

The Byte-Range B

A neo-brutalist letterform 'B' sliced into 4 discrete concurrency byte blocks with a forward velocity ray.

  • Horizontal slice intervals directly express RFC 7233 chunk partitioning
  • Thick ink outlines and hard drop shadow match the frontend dashboard cards
